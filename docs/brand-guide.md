# Christopher Kingwill — provisional brand guide

Status: working system based on the supplied diorama catalog and site mockups.
Chris’s approval is required before public launch.

## Brand idea

**Small worlds. Real natural history.**

Christopher builds miniature museum-inspired habitats that connect patient
handwork, scientific curiosity, and the pleasure of looking closely. The brand
should feel like a natural-history field journal opened inside a contemporary
gallery: warm, intelligent, tactile, and accessible.

## Positioning

Christopher Kingwill is a biogeographer and artist creating one-of-one natural
history dioramas. His work translates living and extinct habitats into small,
carefully built scenes for collectors, educators, museums, and fellow natural
history enthusiasts.

This positioning is grounded in the supplied site copy. It is not an externally
verified résumé or exhibition record.

## Personality

- Curious, never clinical
- Hand-built, never rustic-by-default
- Scientifically literate, never academic in tone
- Warm and inviting, never childish
- Museum-inspired, never institutional

## Visual system

### Color

| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | Field Paper | `#F5ECD9` | Main canvas and editorial space |
| Primary | Specimen Ink | `#2A1F15` | Type, dark sections, image shading |
| Accent | Earth Umber | `#8A4E2F` | Active states and scientific labels |
| Support | Archive Moss | `#526044` | Process/video section and occasional fields |
| Support | Museum Brass | `#B08848` | Rare highlights only |
| Light | Bone | `#FAF4E4` | Type on dark fields and quiet surfaces |

Artwork photography supplies the dominant color. Interface color must stay
quiet enough that greens, clay, resin water, and figurines remain authoritative.

### Typography

- Display: Fraunces for names, section statements, and story-led headlines
- Utility: DM Sans for readable body copy
- Field notes: DM Mono for era, region, fauna, materials, and navigation
- Limit: these three roles only; the monospace face is an information device,
  not decoration

The current build loads fonts from Google. Self-host before launch if the final
privacy, licensing, or performance decision requires it.

### Wordmark

The provisional identity is a circular `CK` field mark beside the full name.
It recalls a specimen label without imitating a museum seal. Do not stretch,
outline, shadow, or lock it inside another shape.

### Photography

- Lead with the strongest close view, then show a complete-object view
- Preserve the original orientation and approved crop
- Do not remove visible handmade edges; material truth is part of the work
- Do not add filters, faux museum frames, AI extensions, or invented habitats
- Keep a consistent neutral color treatment across each series
- Caption every piece with title, availability, era, region, fauna, and materials

## Voice

Use plainspoken wonder backed by concrete detail. A sentence can be playful,
but the facts should remain specific.

- Prefer: “A triceratops moves through a mangrove forest along the Western
  Interior Seaway.”
- Avoid: “A breathtaking journey through the mists of time.”
- Prefer: “Hand-sculpted clay mangroves and reindeer moss.”
- Avoid: “Premium mixed media.”

## Audience pathways

The site should help four audiences act without turning into four separate
brands:

1. Collectors: see full pieces, availability, and commission path
2. Educators and museums: understand scientific framing and outreach potential
3. Natural history enthusiasts: follow the YouTube process and field notes
4. Local arts community: understand the Lafayette/Acadiana connection
